Cruz Castillo

Hello! I am a fifth year PhD student at UIUC in Number Theory. My advisor is Scott Ahlgren. I am also a Sloan Scholar and a NSF graduate research fellow. Next year I will be a VAP at Georgia Tech under the supervision of Alex Dunn. Modular forms, Maass forms, and L-functions are all of great interest to me. I enjoy using a blend of theoretical tools and computations in my research.
